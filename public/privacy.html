Information We Collect

When you create an account or place an order on WatchHub, we collect information such as your name, phone number, delivery address, and email address. This information is used solely to process and deliver your orders.

We use your information to process orders, communicate order updates via WhatsApp or email, manage your account (including wishlist and order history), and improve our website and services.

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website, such as which pages are viewed and how long visitors stay. This helps us improve our store. Analytics data is anonymized and does not directly identify you.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Your order details may be shared with delivery personnel solely for the purpose of fulfilling your order.

Data Security

Your account and order data is stored securely using Google Firebase. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access.

Your Rights

You may update your account information or request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.
