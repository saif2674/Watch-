Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026

Return Window

You may request a return within 7 days of receiving your order, provided the item is unused, undamaged, and in its original packaging.

How to Initiate a Return

To initiate a return, please contact us via WhatsApp or email with your order details and reason for return. We will guide you through the return process.

Non-Returnable Items

Items that show signs of use, damage caused by the customer, or are missing original packaging are not eligible for return.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we will notify you of the approval status. Approved refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days via your original payment method or bank transfer.

Exchanges

If you received a damaged or defective item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery for a free exchange or replacement.

Delivery Charges

Delivery charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (wrong item shipped or defective product).
